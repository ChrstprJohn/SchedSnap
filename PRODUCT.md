# UniToolbox

UniToolbox brings practical tools for university students into one extensible platform. The landing page introduces the platform and links to independent services; it is not branded exclusively around schedule wallpapers.

The first service turns a registration-form image into a mobile schedule wallpaper: choose a template, upload the form, verify the extracted details, then download a PNG. Gemini extraction stays behind a server API. Manual entry is available when extraction fails. Students can correct subjects, times, weekday meetings, and rooms. All edits remain in memory; uploads are not stored.

Current output is 1080 × 2400 portrait. The collection has 42 templates: 20 animal mascot editions and six editable pattern templates appear before the 16 original general templates inspired by the supplied reference images. Bold, soft, and everyday editions offer independent wallpaper personalities while retaining the established white/slate application. The student journey remains choose, review, then download.

The mascot editions pair transparent animal cutouts with uniform Canvas backgrounds. No timetable, student details, heading, or edition label is baked into the animal art; the renderer supplies them separately. All templates share a readable Class Schedule title, separate growing day cards, and consistent time and subject columns. Circular M, T, W, TH, F badges stay centered within their day; times include minutes and AM/PM and center beside each meeting. Actual schedules preserve every meeting, keep subject names on one line, shrink them by up to 20%, then truncate with an ellipsis, and omit empty days. Body type fits within 28–42px. Mascots retain a fixed 620px maximum occupied dimension in a reserved footer; schedules that still overflow block export. Gallery examples show Monday–Friday with one neutral subject and time each, and images load near the viewport. Device, tablet, and orientation controls are outside the current scope.

The interface should be clean, white, and concise. Workflow steps belong inside the tool page, not the site navigation. Template previews open in a modal. Step 2 contains upload, class editing, and confirmation. Step 3 contains only the large actual wallpaper preview and PNG download. There is no custom wallpaper title input.

Course codes lead each meeting in large bold type, with smaller centered subject names below and a separate centered room column. Missing codes fall back to a readable subject name. Template previews include a left-side appearance panel on desktop, inside a collapsible panel above the preview on phones: background, day-container, text and separator colors, plus DM Sans, Geist, Poppins, Inter, Roboto, Open Sans, Montserrat, Lato, DM Serif Display or Georgia. Choices apply live, and Use template retains them for the final preview and PNG. Reset appearance restores the original preset. Settings stay in page memory. Existing schedules appear in the template modal; otherwise neutral weekday examples show the same code/subject/room hierarchy through the same renderer.
