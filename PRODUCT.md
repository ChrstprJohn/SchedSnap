# SchedSnap

SchedSnap is a dedicated class schedule wallpaper creator for university students. A static creamy hero fills the first viewport, layering a transparent three-phone foreground over a separate generated ambient background. Concise live copy sits on the left, beside a bounded phone group on the right. Phones and tablets stack the same centered 4:3 foreground beneath the copy, while short landscape viewports use compact columns. A full-width warm cream statistics row softly fades in from the hero through a feathered 32–72px blur transition and shows 12K+ total visits, 62+ templates and 4+ collections. Visits remain a mock value without a visible sample label, as requested; template and collection counts still derive from actual definitions. All three counters ease from zero to their totals over 1.2 seconds when at least a quarter of the statistics row first enters view. The animation runs once per page visit and does not replay when scrolling back. Reduced-motion users see final totals immediately, and screen readers receive stable full-number totals. The homepage does not call the optional Redis visit counter while using mock data. Its open ordered steps use three columns on desktop and stack vertically on phones, immediately before Find your style. Desktop alternates artwork left/copy right, copy left/artwork right, then artwork left/copy right; smaller screens stack. Each row has a five-second carousel with hover, focus, hidden-page, offscreen and reduced-motion gates. Fixed copy is For mobile., For tablet. and For laptop., with concise descriptions and View more. These actions open /wallpapers/mobile, /wallpapers/tablet and /wallpapers/laptop galleries, each with Little Friends, Mascots, Patterns and Originals. Mobile links continue to the actual editor. Tablet images combine portrait and landscape tablets in every scene, with distinct poses and wallpapers; tablet and phone share the 460px picture width and 560px height caps and column proportion. Below Find your style, a compact horizontal maker banner pairs a tan capybara with Thanks for stopping by. The banner uses this static image without mouse tracking or separate eye layers. Its scenes are illustrative previews; the editor uses actual Canvas-rendered schedules.

The creator turns a registration-form image into a mobile schedule wallpaper: choose a template, upload the form, verify the extracted details, then download a PNG. Gemini extraction stays behind a server API. Manual entry is available when extraction fails. Students can correct subjects, times, weekday meetings, and rooms. All edits remain in memory; uploads are not stored. A native browser confirmation protects unsaved changes when refreshing or leaving the page, without a persistent refresh-reset note.

Current output is 1080 × 2400 portrait. The collection has 62 templates: 20 Chiikawa-inspired Little Friends editions, 20 animal mascot editions and six editable pattern templates appear before the 16 original general templates inspired by the supplied reference images. Bold, soft, and everyday editions offer independent wallpaper personalities while retaining the established white/slate application. The student journey remains choose, review, then download.

The mascot editions pair transparent animal cutouts with uniform Canvas backgrounds. No timetable, student details, heading, or edition label is baked into the animal art; the renderer supplies them separately. All templates share a readable Class Schedule title, separate growing day cards, and consistent time and subject columns. Circular M, T, W, TH, F badges stay centered within their day; times include minutes and AM/PM and center beside each meeting. Actual schedules preserve every meeting, keep subject names on one line, shrink them by up to 20%, then truncate with an ellipsis, and omit empty days. Body type fits within 28–42px. Mascots retain a fixed 620px maximum occupied dimension in a reserved footer; schedules that still overflow block export. Gallery examples show Monday–Friday with one neutral subject and time each, and images load near the viewport. Tablet and laptop artwork and browse galleries are available; tablet/laptop editor formats and orientation controls remain outside the current scope.
