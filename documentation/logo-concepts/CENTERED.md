# Centered mascot concepts

User requested centered characters for the top navigation and replaced the book and backpack directions. This batch contains two student buddies, two study owls, and two campus cats with graduation caps. All prompts request the whole character centered with even padding and no corner cropping. These are static previews; navigation integration and animation are pending selection.

| Labels | Product connection | Subject colors | Backgrounds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1, A2 | Student buddies for the college audience | A1 ivory/navy; A2 teal/navy | A1 pale blue; A2 cream |
| B1, B2 | Study owls for learning and academic tools | B1 cream/navy; B2 teal/navy | B1 pale sage; B2 cream |
| C1, C2 | Campus cats as friendly student companions | C1 ivory/navy; C2 terracotta/navy | C1 pale blue; C2 pale sage |

All six are native 1254 × 1254 PNG images generated through the built-in image generation tool. The runtime did not disclose a model identifier. Constraints were delivered through the main prompt. Each candidate was generated once and preserved without post-processing. A1 and A2 are referenced edits of the original students; B1, B2, C1, and C2 are independent new generations. Exact prompts, palette hex values, paths, and metadata are in [centered-campus.json](centered-campus.json), [centered-owls.json](centered-owls.json), and [centered-cats.json](centered-cats.json).
